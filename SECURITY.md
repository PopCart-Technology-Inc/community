# Security

**Please do not report security problems in public issues.**

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**), or email support.popcart@gmail.com with "SECURITY" in the subject.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. We will reply to confirm we received it, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you if you want credit.

There is no running service yet. Until there is, reports about the design (for example, a weakness in how credentials are signed or verified) are very welcome.
