# Code of conduct

PopCart Community is for people who work, from every trade and every place. Treat everyone here the way you would want to be treated at your own workplace.

## Do

- Be respectful, patient and generous, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Write so others can understand you. English and Filipino are both welcome.

## Do not

- Harass, insult, threaten or demean anyone, for any reason.
- Share anyone's personal information without their consent.
- Post sexual content involving minors. This is reported to the authorities, always (RA 11930).
- Spam, scam or advertise.

## Reporting

Email support.popcart@gmail.com with "CONDUCT" in the subject. Reports are handled privately. People who break these rules may be warned, have their posts removed, or be blocked from the project.
